# Scholarship map and boundary

White Line's scholarship is intentionally plural. The manuscript does not
claim that feminist epistemology, epistemologies of ignorance, missing-data
statistics, critical data studies, privacy theory, epistemic injustice,
Indigenous refusal, and apophatic philosophy are one school. It asks what each
contributes to a small review instrument and where the translation must stop.

| Lineage | Useful contribution | White Line's boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situated knowledge and strong objectivity | Knowledge is partial and located; accountability about position can strengthen inquiry rather than destroy objectivity. | The package records date and provenance, not reviewer positionality, power, or standpoint. |
| Epistemologies of ignorance, agnotology, and strategic ignorance | Ignorance can be produced, sustained, racialized, campaigned for, or held off as uncomfortable knowledge an institution cannot absorb. | The package records a gap; it does not infer who produced it, why, or who benefits, and cannot tell a manufactured gap from an ordinary one. |
| Missing-data methodology | Inference depends on assumptions about the process generating missingness; observed data cannot adjudicate MNAR against its MAR counterpart, and partial identification reports bounds instead of a point estimate. | The package does not impute values, estimate MCAR, MAR, MNAR, or a causal mechanism, or report a bound of any kind. |
| Classification and data feminism | Categories and data infrastructures shape visibility, power, and whose work counts. | The registry is inspectable and versioned; its categories remain finite, situated, and non-exhaustive. |
| Epistemic injustice and resistance | People can be wronged as knowers; resistance concerns the social conditions of participation and shared responsibility. | `WITHHELD` and `NOT_RECORDED` are not diagnoses of deception, injustice, intent, or authority. |
| Refusal, damage-centered research, and contextual integrity | Completion can expose or reproduce harm; information flow must fit context, sovereignty, and consent. | Follow-up honors boundaries and asks human questions rather than demanding disclosure. |
| Apophatic and contemplative restraint | A claim can be disciplined by refusing to reify what exceeds its scope; apophatic language performs a regress of unsaying and reports no inner experience. | Negative space is not evidence of an invisible force, substance, or agent, and a contemplative record performs no regress and reports no experience. |

The operational synthesis is deliberately modest: **locate the record, type the
limit, model or decline the mechanism, protect refusal, and bound/revisit the
claim**. Only the last part is partly automated. The current bibliography
records the edition or DOI used for each claim; `manuscript/01c_scholarship.md`
carries the long-form discussion and the generated `white_scholarship_map`
figure. Scholarship constrains the design; it does not validate the package or
turn a state label into an empirical finding.
